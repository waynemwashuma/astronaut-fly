# Astronaut Fly

Astronaut Fly is a fast, arcade-style space game about keeping an astronaut in motion for as long as possible. The experience is simple to jump into: launch, collect, react quickly, and try to push your score a little higher each run.

## What It Is

Astronaut Fly is built around short runs that are easy to replay.Set against a starry backdrop, the game puts you in control of a drifting astronaut who needs careful timing to stay moving in the right direction. The pace is light at first, then quickly becomes a test of rhythm, focus and recovery.

## How To Play

Start the game from the opening screen.
Tap or press the space bar or screen to boost the astronaut.
Collect stars to increase your score.
Pick up boosters to keep your momentum going.
If your run ends, choose to play again or return to the main screen.
